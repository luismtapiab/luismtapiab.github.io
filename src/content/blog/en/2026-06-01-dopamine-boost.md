Here is my [little wheel of dopamine](https://luismtapiab.github.io/dopamine-daily-boost/).

<iframe src="https://luismtapiab.github.io/dopamine-daily-boost/" width="100%" height="600px" style="border:none;"></iframe>

Whitout going into detail of my entire journey about dopamine seeking, detoxing and whatnot, this is part of my experiments to improve my focus and productivity.

I'm trying something that help me get into the flow state more often. But it is not an engine that can start on cold. It requires some lubrication to get it going. I'm trying the technique of using a boost early in the day, which is part of what other people call a dopamine menu. 

Anyway, the idea is to do something that you enjoy, but not too long, early in the day then the need is eased and you can focus on important tasks knowing you have not ignored yourself. 

If this wheel is liked enough I might add more options to it, it already includes:
- Tracking of your progress, 
- Registering falls and reflection on your journey
- Custom activities (which means you can add your own).

I'm open to suggestions for more features to include. I considered shared activities to be voted on by all users, but I'm not sure if I want to add that much complexity to it. 

Right now it is all client-side so there is nothing to worry about privacy-wise, and in the case I decide to go further It will be anonymized (thats why you'll see a generated code on the navbar).
